Privacy Policy for Plant Pot Measurer

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Plant Pot Measurer ("the app") is an Android application that helps users measure the dimensions and volume of plant pots using their device's camera. This policy explains how the app handles data.

Summary

Plant Pot Measurer does not collect, transmit, or share any personal or device data with the developer or any third party. The app has no server, no account system, no analytics, and no advertising. Everything the app creates or stores stays only on your device.

What the app stores, and where

All data below is stored locally on your device only, using standard Android app storage. None of it is ever transmitted anywhere.

  • Photos you take or select within the app, used to measure a pot's dimensions.
  • Saved reference objects you choose to save (for example, a custom object and its length) for reuse in future measurements.
  • Measurement history — a record of past pots you've measured, so you can look back at them later.
  • Calibration settings and unit preference (centimeters or inches) you configure within the app.

Permissions the app requests

  • Camera — used only to take photos within the app for measurement purposes. Photos are not uploaded anywhere.
  • Photo library / storage access — used only if you choose to pick an existing photo instead of taking a new one.

Third parties

The app does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services. It does not share data with any third party, because it does not transmit data anywhere in the first place.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, since it does not collect information from anyone at all.

Data deletion

Because everything is stored locally, uninstalling the app permanently deletes all data the app has stored (photos, history, saved references, and settings) from your device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in the future, the update will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
